The Swatch Book

What
Thirty-seven small games, one for each art style in Game Art Style, a visual atlas of how games see the world. Each game's idea grows out of its style: watercolour bleeds, so its puzzle is bleeding; a vase turns, so its battle goes round it.
Made with
Rust and WebGPU, one shared kit, and Claude Code: one session designed the games and the kit, and builder agents made one game each, playing every one in a headless browser before it was done.
Plays on
Phones first (portrait, touch), and computers with a keyboard. Needs WebGPU: Chrome on Android and computers, Edge, Safari 26+ on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
Saves
Best scores stay in this browser. Mute once and every game stays quiet.
Care
Play in short bursts. Do not tumble dry. Return to Neri's Game Post on lelelilo.com for more letters.
